# 🌱 GreenStock – How to Deploy Your App

## What you need (all FREE):
1. A computer (Windows or Mac)
2. Internet connection
3. 15 minutes of your time

---

## STEP 1 — Install Node.js
Go to: https://nodejs.org
Download and install the "LTS" version (big green button).

---

## STEP 2 — Set up the project
1. Open the folder you downloaded (nursery-app)
2. Open a terminal / command prompt inside that folder
   - Windows: Right-click inside the folder → "Open in Terminal"
   - Mac: Right-click → "New Terminal at Folder"
3. Type this and press Enter:
   npm install

Wait for it to finish (1-2 minutes).

---

## STEP 3 — Deploy to Vercel (FREE hosting)
1. Go to: https://vercel.com
2. Click "Sign Up" → choose "Continue with Google" (use your Gmail)
3. After logging in, click "Add New → Project"
4. Click "Browse" and select your nursery-app folder
5. Click "Deploy" — wait 2 minutes
6. You will get a link like: https://greenstock-xyz.vercel.app

---

## STEP 4 — Install on your phone
1. Open the link on your phone in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone)
2. Android: Tap the 3 dots menu → "Add to Home Screen"
3. iPhone: Tap the Share button (box with arrow) → "Add to Home Screen"
4. The app icon appears on your home screen like a real app! ✅

---

## STEP 5 — Share with staff
Just send them the Vercel link (e.g. https://greenstock-xyz.vercel.app)
They can install it on their phones the same way (Step 4).

---

## Your data is SAVED automatically
- All inventory changes save in the browser automatically
- Each device keeps its own data (no internet needed after install)
